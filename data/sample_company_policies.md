# Enterprise Operations & Human Resources Policy Manual

## 1. Onboarding and Probation
All new full-time employees undergo a mandatory probation period of **three (3) months** starting from their official start date. A mid-probation performance review is conducted at **60 days** by the direct manager. Upon successful completion of the three-month period, the employee transitions to permanent status with standard company benefits.

## 2. Remote Work and Home Office Allowance
Employees approved for hybrid or remote work are eligible for a one-time home office setup reimbursement of up to **$500 USD**. Eligible purchases include ergonomic chairs, external monitors, keyboards, mice, and desk accessories. Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal within 45 days of hire.

## 3. Annual Leave and Rollover
Full-time employees receive **25 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, accrued monthly. Employees are encouraged to take all accrued leave within the year. A maximum of **5 unused vacation days** may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year, expiring on March 31st if not taken.

## 4. Security and Compliance Policy
Information security is paramount. All employees must configure **Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA / 2FA)** on all corporate accounts, including email and internal systems. Corporate passwords must be changed every **90 days** and cannot match any of the previous 5 passwords. Unattended workstations must be locked immediately.

## 5. Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement
When traveling for company business, employees receive a daily meal allowance (per diem) of up to **$75 USD**. Receipts are strictly required for any individual expense item exceeding **$25 USD**. All travel bookings must be approved at least 14 days in advance by department directors.
